About Nyaya Setu

An initiative of the Department of Justice to make court records and services accessible to every citizen.

Mission & Vision

Justice, accessible to all

Our Mission

Nyaya Setu aims to bridge the gap between citizens and the judicial system by providing a unified, transparent, and efficient digital platform. We strive to make court services available 24x7, reducing the need for physical courthouse visits and simplifying complex legal processes for both litigants and advocates.

The Vision

We envision a future where technology empowers justice. By integrating case management, e-filing, and real-time updates across thousands of district and subordinate courts, we are building a seamless ecosystem that ensures swift, equitable, and accountable judicial services nationwide.
